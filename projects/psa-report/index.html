Executive Production & Sales Performance Reporting

A multi-page Power BI solution built to bring production, commission, portfolio, sales conversion, loss analysis, pending business and target-versus-actual performance into a single management reporting environment.

Project Type Business Intelligence
Primary Tool Microsoft Power BI
Focus Performance & Decision Support
Role Design, Modeling & Reporting

Business problem

Management reporting required several operational and financial perspectives to be analyzed together rather than through isolated reports.

Fragmented performance views

Production, commissions, portfolio development, sales outcomes and target performance needed to be viewed through a consistent reporting structure.

Need for drill-down analysis

High-level KPIs alone were not sufficient. Users needed the ability to move from executive summaries into branches, producers, insurance companies, customers and individual transaction-level detail.

The solution

I developed a structured Power BI reporting environment that connects management-level KPIs with detailed operational analysis.

  • Production & Portfolio Performance Net premium, commissions, customer volumes, portfolio sources and performance-year analysis.
  • Commission Analysis Commission values and commission rates by performance period, insurance branch and portfolio source.
  • Hit Ratio Analysis Monthly and cumulative conversion performance for internal and external business by insurance branch.
  • Loss Reason Analysis Analysis of unsuccessful sales by reason, transaction count and premium value, supported by detailed drill-down reporting.
  • Pending Sales Monitoring Transaction-level visibility into business still waiting for a sales outcome.
  • Major Won & Lost Business Visibility into high-value renewals and material sales outcomes requiring management attention.
  • Target versus Actual Performance Comparison of premium and commission targets against actual performance across insurance branches.

Capabilities demonstrated

The project combines financial understanding, data modeling and business intelligence development rather than focusing only on dashboard visualization.
